1. What it Does: It is a CLI program that will have the exact same functionality as Zetamac (basic arithmetic with
integer ranges). What it will be doing on top of that is actually logging every answer, pattern recognition (questions 
that are repeatedly incorrect, ones that take longer, etc.). With this pattern recognition, it will be prioritizing 
repeating those and assessing whether there improving, and then self-iterate based on those results. 
2. Example Session: An example session could look like roughly the same, simple UI as zetamac, but perhaps a "hint" button that reveals 
the least significant digit? Sometimes your brain is just stuck and instead of getting more problems in, you just 
freeze on that one problem and the time goes away. However, for problems that a hint is used on, those are tracked 
as weak problems along with all the other criteria for a weak problem that needs to be repeated.
3. V0 Features: Basic Zetamac functionality and UI.
4. What Gets Recorded: I've explained in previous my previous answers in this spec. In addition to that, perhaps even 
expressions that you're really good at/consistently get right, perhaps even how far off you are on most problems, 
common mistakes like adding to many for these problems, and milliseconds taken perhaps, but maybe that's too small of a
timeframe because usually its on the order of seconds.
5. Weakest?: I feel ive explained this already
6. Out of Scope for V0: Fancy UI (its a CLI so yk, not much we can do), the hint feature
7. Classes: Answer (maybe an interface?), WeakAnswer, StrongAnswer, Calculator
